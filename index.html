Pump It Up!

Pump clean water to the village before time runs out.

  • Tap the pump to send water through the pipe and fill the tank.
  • Fill enough tanks before time runs out to reach the village goal.
  • Watch out for leaks! They waste water and cost you points if you don't fix them in time.
